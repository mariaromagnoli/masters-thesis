WHY NOT 'WITHIN BATCH'
within-batch correlation between the treatment and the worker's OWN urban
share, across batches with at least 3 workers:
  mean -1 | min -1 | max -1 | batches 36 
A correlation of -1 means the two are the same variable inside a batch, so
batch fixed effects leave nothing of the treatment behind.


WHY NOT THE LEAVE-OUT POOL MEAN
The firm leave-out mean was considered as the control for the leave-out
bias. Within a firm it is an exact linear function of the worker's own
urban share, so it cannot serve as an independent control:
   firm workers  r
 Firm A     254 -1
 Firm B     269 -1
 Firm C      81 -1
 Firm E      30 -1
 Firm F      10 -1
Firm fixed effects are used instead.
